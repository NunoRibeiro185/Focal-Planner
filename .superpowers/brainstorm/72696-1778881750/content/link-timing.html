When does the client get the deal room link?

The link timing shapes the whole experience. When should the client first be able to access their deal room?

A

Right after they submit the inquiry

The moment an inquiry is submitted, the system creates a deal room and emails the client their link. At first the page only shows their inquiry summary. The proposal section is empty until the photographer sends one.

Client experience: "You'll hear from us soon — here's your booking page where you can track everything."
B

When the photographer sends the proposal

The photographer builds the proposal, then hits "Send to client." That action generates the deal room link and sends it via email. The client's first visit is seeing the full proposal — nothing empty.

Client experience: "Here's your photography proposal — review and let us know."
C

Photographer decides manually each time

The photographer has a "Share with client" button in the booking detail. They can share at any point — after inquiry, after proposal, or both. They control exactly what's visible when they share.

Client experience: Depends on when the photographer shares and what they've set up.
